What we treat

Pests of every kind, handled naturally

Through our certified entomologist’s experience, we design a pest solution to alleviate your pest concerns — and keep them from coming back.

Rodents

Mice, Norway rats, voles, and other rodents can sneak into your home and cause serious damage. We offer help for removal and prevention.

Wasps & Hornets

Wasps, hornets, and yellowjackets can be dangerous when they nest too close for comfort. We identify and remove nests safely.

Ants

Ants invade your home for food, warmth, and shelter, often multiplying fast. Our associate entomologist can identify the insect and offer a solution.

Bed Bugs

Bed bugs hide in mattresses, furniture, and cracks, and multiply fast. We identify them and offer a solution to eliminate and prevent them.

German Cockroaches

German cockroaches invade kitchens and bathrooms for food and warmth. We offer a solution to eliminate them and keep them from coming back.

General Pest Control

Seasonal pests and general infestations in your home or business. We design a solution for whatever is bothering you, and keep it from coming back.
